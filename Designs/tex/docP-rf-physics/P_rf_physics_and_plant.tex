``replaced'' the AB hardware, is incorrect and has been amended.

\begin{keynote}
This document is implementation-independent. It states the physics of the
SPEAR3 RF plant and the control problem that follows from it: what disturbs the
cavity field, over what frequency range, and which loop is responsible for each
band. Hardware-specific detail belongs in Doc~L (legacy) and Doc~0 (upgrade).
\end{keynote}
