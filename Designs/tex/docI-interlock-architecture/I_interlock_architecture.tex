\vspace{4mm}
\begin{keynote}
This document traces every interlock path in the \emph{legacy} SPEAR3 RF station:
what trips, how fast, through which hardware, and what the software can and
cannot see. It is the reference against which the upgraded protection chain in
Doc~0 is justified. Where the two disagree, the upgrade document states the
intended future behaviour and this document states the present one.
\end{keynote}
