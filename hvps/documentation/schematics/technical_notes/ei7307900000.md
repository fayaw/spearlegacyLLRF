## 1. ⚠ This drawing settles the transformer rating

The input block reads, verbatim:

> **INPUT**
> **2750 KVA 3 PHASE**
> **12.5 KV AT 127 AMPS RMS**

The two figures are **internally consistent**: $2750\,\text{kVA} / (\sqrt{3} \times 12.5\,\text{kV}) = 127.0\,\text{A}$.

### Comparison with the other figures in circulation

| Source | Figure | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **EI-730-790-00-C0 (this drawing)** | **2750 kVA, 127 A at 12.5 kV** | **Manufacturer's nameplate. Authoritative.** |
| PS-341-360-01 §1.5 | "Nominal phase shifting transformer power 350 kVA" | Far too small to pass 2.5 MW — almost certainly a decimal/typographical error |
| SD-730-790-01-C1 | "3000 KVA" | A round figure, close to but not equal to the nameplate |
| J. Sebek, `spear3HvpsHazards.tex` | Adopts **3.5 MVA**, derives 162 A/phase | A reasoned estimate made without access to this drawing; the 162 A follows arithmetically from assuming 3.5 MVA |

> **Use 2750 kVA / 127 A.** Sebek's 162 A/phase is a consequence of his 3.5 MVA assumption, not an independent measurement, and should be superseded. His "~113 A typical" operating figure remains reasonable — it is below the 127 A rating, as expected at a ~2.5 MW operating point.

## 2. Main power path and output taps

```
INPUT 12.5 kV 3Ø, 2750 kVA, 127 A RMS
   H1 ──┬── 1CT 300:5 ──┐
   H2 ──┼── 2CT 300:5 ──┼──►  T0  PHASE SHIFTING TRANSFORMER
   H3 ──┴── 3CT 300:5 ──┘      (X1/X2/X3 and Y1/Y2/Y3 outputs)
                                      │
                    ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
                    ▼                                   ▼
              T1 RECTIFIER TRANS                  T2 RECTIFIER TRANS
              (H1–H6, A1/X1, B1/Y1,               (H1–H6, A1/X1, B1/Y1,
               A2/X2, B2/Y2, A3/X3, B3/Y3)         A2/X2, B2/Y2, A3/X3, B3/Y3)
                    │                                   │
                    ▼                                   ▼
            [SCR CONTROLLER — SUPPLIED BY CUSTOMER]
                    │                                   │
              L1 CHOKE                            L2 CHOKE
                    │                                   │
                    └──────────► rectifier stacks ◄─────┘
                                       │
                          [SCR CROWBAR — SUPPLIED BY CUSTOMER]
                                       │
        ┌──────────┬──────────┬────────┴───────┬──────────┐
      −90 kV     −77 kV     −52 kV           −26 kV      GND
```

The cumulative tap sequence **GND / −26 kV / −52 kV / −77 kV / −90 kV** matches SD-730-790-01-C1 exactly. As noted throughout this repository, **−77 kV is an intermediate tap, not the operating point**.

> The drawing labels L1 and L2 as **CHOKE**, not "filter inductor". They are the same devices.

## 3. Protection switches and setpoints

Read directly from the device block at bottom left. These are the **manufacturer's setpoints** and are the authoritative values.

| Device | Type | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Vacuum switch | Pressure/vacuum, shown at zero pressure (ATM) | **Set @ 7.0 PSIG**, adjustable 0.75–9.0 PSIG (increasing vacuum) |
| Pressure switch | Pressure/vacuum | **Set @ 7.0 PSIG**, adjustable 1.0–12.0 PSIG (rising pressure) |
| Oil over-temperature switch | DPDT | **Set @ 75 °C** |
| Oil over-temperature switch | DPDT | **Set @ 90 °C** |
| Oil low-level switch | SPDT, shown at 25 °C level | **Low @ 3" below 25 °C level** |
| Pressure relief device | DPDT | **Set @ 5 PSI** |
| Pressure relief device | DPDT | **Set @ 5 PSI** |
| Oil pump motor | **1MTR, ½ HP** | Requires **115 V AC @ 10 A** |
| Oil flow switch | **1FLS**, SPDT, shown in no-flow condition | **Make 10 GPM / break 10 GPM** |

### Contact ratings (as drawn)

- General switches: **15 A @ 125, 250 and 480 V AC**; **0.5 A @ 125 V DC**
- Oil flow switch: **17 A @ 125 V AC**

> There are **two** oil over-temperature switches at different setpoints (75 °C and 90 °C) and **two** pressure relief devices, giving a staged alarm/trip arrangement. Derived notes elsewhere generally mention only one of each.

## 4. Monitor windings — the source of the Enerpro phase references and TS-7

Terminal block **1TB** brings out current transformers and monitor windings:

| 1TB terminals | Signal | Belongs to |
|---|---|---|
| 21, 22 | **1CT (H1 phase)**, 300:5 | Input |
| 23, 24 | **2CT (H2 phase)**, 300:5 | Input |
| 25, 26 | **3CT (H3 phase)**, 300:5 | Input |
| 27–30 | Monitor windings (A phase, B phase) | **Phase shifting transformer T0** |
| 31–34 | Monitor windings (A phase, C phase) | **Rectifier transformer T1** |
| 35–38 | Monitor windings (B phase, C phase) | **Rectifier transformer T2** |
| 39, 40 | Monitor winding | **Choke L1** |
| 41, 42 | Monitor winding | **Choke L2** |
| 43, 44, 45 | Spare | — |

Terminals 1–20 carry the protection switch contacts as N.O./N.C. pairs.

### 4.1 These map directly onto TS-7 in the Hoffman Box

WD-730-790-01-C3 labels the TS-7 "TRANSFORMER MONITORS" signals as LINE VOLTAGE A/B/C, 1 XFORMER A/B/C, 2 XFORMER A/B/C, 1 INDUCTOR and 2 INDUCTOR. Those correspond one-for-one to the T0, T1, T2, L1 and L2 monitor windings above.

### 4.2 These are the Enerpro phase references

J. Sebek records that the Enerpro FCOG6100 phase references come from *"monitor windings on the phase-shifting transformer legs"* at ≈190 V peak-to-peak with a 500 Ω source impedance. This drawing shows those windings explicitly on **T0**, brought out at **1TB-27…30**. See `hvps/documentation/schematics/technical_notes/sd2372301299.md` for how they are scaled into the FCOG6100 (R37–R39 changed from the standard 1.9 MΩ to 200 kΩ at SLAC).
