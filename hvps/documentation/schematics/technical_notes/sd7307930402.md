## 2. Notes on the Drawing — transcribed verbatim

> **1. This board is used to drive the new optical SCR.**
>
> **2. For the old conventional SCR, replace resistors R3 and R37 with 78.70K, 1% Tol.**

These two notes are the most operationally significant content on the sheet. They record that the crowbar was **converted from conventional to optically triggered SCRs**, and that the same board serves either type with a two-resistor change. Anyone working on this board must first establish which SCR type is installed.

## 3. Timing — from the drawing's own timing diagram

The timing diagram is captioned *"with Single-Shot Trigger Input from The Test Box"*:

| Test point | Interval |
|---|---|
| **TP1** | **50 ms (Adj. by R1)** |
| **TP2** | **5 ms** |
| **TP3** | **44 µs (Adj. by R2)** |
| **TP4 – TP5** ("Optical SCR Trigger") | **5 µs**, **5 µs**, **5 µs** |

> Note the timescale contrast with the 12 kV SCR Driver Board (SD-730-793-03), whose envelope is 50 **µs**. The crowbar board's 50 **ms** envelope holds the crowbar SCRs hard on long enough to carry the full fault discharge and to guarantee the primary breaker has time to interrupt (4–8 ms per SLAC-PUB-7591).

## 6. Timing Chain

| Stage | Device | Label | Timing components |
|---|---|---|---|
| Envelope astable | **U1 CD4047B** ("ASTBLE") | **"15 – 60 mS"** | **R1 100 kΩ 12-turn**, R18 24.9 kΩ, C13 0.1 µF, C23 0.1 µF → TP1 |
| Carrier astable | **U4 CD4047B** ("ASTBLE") | **"20 ~ 120 µS"** | **R2 100 kΩ 12-turn**, R19 24.9 kΩ, C2 200 pF, C11 0.1 µF → TP3 |
| Monostable | **U6B CD4538** | **"5 mS"** | R38 49.9 kΩ, C5 0.1 µF, C14 0.1 µF → TP2 |
| Monostable | **U6A CD4538** | **"5 µS"** | **R37 24.9 kΩ**, C1 200 pF |
| Monostable | **U2A CD4538** | **"5 µS"** | R4 24.9 kΩ, C4 200 pF |
| Monostable | **U2B CD4538** | **"5 µS"** | **R3 24.9 kΩ**, C3 200 pF, C10 0.1 µF |
| Logic | **U3A–U3F CD4049** inverters | — | — |

> R3 and R37 — the two resistors Note 2 says to change to 78.70 kΩ for conventional SCRs — are the timing resistors of the U2B and U6A 5 µs monostables. Changing them from 24.9 kΩ to 78.70 kΩ lengthens those pulses by roughly 3×, because a conventional SCR gate needs a longer, higher-energy pulse than an optically triggered device.

Indicators: **LED1 HLMP-1700 "TRIGGER"** (R16 2.2 kΩ), LED3 HLMP-1700 (R20 2.2 kΩ). Test points TP1–TP7.

### 7.2 Slave-board output — J1

A second output section drives **J1 (SIP4)**, labelled on the drawing:

> **"Output Plug to Old Slave Trigger Bd"**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Drivers | **U9A, U9B — MIC-4427** |
| Series resistors | R12, R13 — 100 Ω |
| Decoupling | C21 10 µF, C24 0.1 µF |

This is the path by which one crowbar trigger board fires additional slave boards, so that all four crowbar SCR stacks fire together. It corresponds to the "SLAVE CB TRIG" and "SLAVE CB OFF" signals carried on the P1 command bus between the Right Side and Left Side Trigger Interconnect Boards.
