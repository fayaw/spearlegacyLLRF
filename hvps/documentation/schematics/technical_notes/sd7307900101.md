> **Verification status: VERIFIED.** Read directly from the scanned drawing on 3 September 2026 by rendering `hvps/documentation/schematics/sd7307900101.pdf` to image and reading it. Text in quotation marks is transcribed literally from the drawing.
>
> This note replaces two earlier notes for the same drawing (`sd7307900101.md` and `SD-7307900101_HVPS_System_Schematic_Analysis.md`), both of which were written **without reading the drawing**. Between them they contained a fabricated "350 µH + 30 nF filter network", a non-existent "klystron 50 kV rating", a "60 kV 80 A crowbar", an incorrect "cathode grounded configuration", a mis-assignment of T1/T2 as the phase-shifting and isolation transformers, and boilerplate about commercial-grade temperature ranges, ±1 µs timing accuracy and 1500 V isolation that has no counterpart on a power schematic.

### 2.2 DC stage taps

The output section is drawn as **four rectifier/filter stages in series**. Cumulative tap voltages labelled on the drawing, from ground upward:

**GND → −26 kV → −52 kV → −77 kV → −90 kV**

> **−77 kV is an intermediate stage tap, not the klystron operating voltage.** Both superseded notes — and, until September 2026, several documents in `Designs/` — quoted −77 kV as the operating point. The klystron block is labelled 90 kV / 27 A, which is the *supply rating*. The actual operating point is typically −72 to −75 kV at 19–22 A and is trimmed continuously by the HVPS supervisory loop.

Per-stage increments implied by the taps are approximately 26 / 26 / 25 / 13 kV. Sebek's hazards note works the stored-energy calculation with 26 / 26 / 26 / 13 kV summing to 91 kV; the difference is rounding on the drawing labels.

## 3. Cross-checks Against Other Original Sources

| Drawing value | Independent corroboration |
|---|---|
| 8 µF **per stage**, 4 stages | Sebek computes filter stored energy as ½ · 8 µF · (3·26² + 13²)·10⁶ = **8,788 J**, which is only consistent with 8 µF per stage, not 8 µF total |
| 500 Ω / 1 kW filter resistors | PS-341-360-01 §3.8: "8 resistors … rated at 500 ohms and 1 kW in oil" — two per stage |
| 30 kV / 30 A main rectifiers | PS-341-360-01 §3.4 |
| 30 kV / >3 A filter rectifiers | PS-341-360-01 §3.5 |
| "CROWBAR 40KV 80A" | This is the **per-stack device rating** — one crowbar stack across each ~26 kV stage. Separately, PS-341-360-01 §5.3 rates the crowbar oil-to-oil bushings at **100 kV DC with 80 kA peak current**, and §8.1 limits crowbar discharge current to <80 kA by transformer impedance. These three numbers describe different things and must not be conflated. |
| "3000 KVA" input | **Resolved September 2026.** NWL's own schematic EI-730-790-00-C0 (NWL #39308) gives the nameplate as **"INPUT 2750 KVA 3 PHASE / 12.5 KV AT 127 AMPS RMS"**, which is internally consistent (2750 kVA ÷ (√3 × 12.5 kV) = 127 A). The "3000 KVA" on this sheet is a rounded label; PS-341-360-01 §1.5's "350 kVA" is a decimal error; Sebek's 3.5 MVA was a reasoned estimate made without the NWL drawing. **Use 2750 kVA.** See `ei7307900000.md`. |
