## RF Station Safety Certification Check-Off List

Check-off performed by PEP-II RF Group or RF Maintenance Group.

A RF station safety check must be performed after initial installation and after major work on a Station involving the power components such as klystron, waveguide or cavities or work on the interlock system.

The following items need to be checked off:

| Item | Check-Off |
|------|-----------|
| **WAVEGUIDE FLANGE TORQUE TEST:** Check torque on six random bolts >25 ft-lbs on all accessible flanges upstairs and tunnel — see RF Non-ionizing Radiation Procedure | ☐ |
| **INTERLOCK TEST:** Check all interlocks for proper functioning — see Interlock Test Procedure | ☐ |
| **KLYSTRON GROUNDING CABLE** connected | ☐ |
| **KLYSTRON HV CABLE** connected with 4 screws in place | ☐ |
| **KLYSTRON LEAD SHIELDING PANELS** and lead shielding at collector in place | ☐ |

The station is now ready to be turned ON.

---

After station turn-on at a klystron output power level of approximately 100 kW a **RF RADIATION SURVEY** using a calibrated RF field meter has to be performed. The RF radiation level must be below **0.1 mW/cm²** anywhere around the accessible waveguide especially any joint.
