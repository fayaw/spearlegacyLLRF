## Radiation Surveys at 100 kW

With the RF station turned on at a klystron output power level of approximately 100 kW, a RF non-ionizing radiation survey using a calibrated RF field meter is performed wherever access dictates. The RF radiation level must be below **0.1 mW/cm²** at every accessible waveguide joint. In addition, the RP should perform an ionizing radiation survey around the klystron to check if sufficient lead shielding is installed to keep radiation below **5 mR/hr at 30 cm** away from any surface of the klystron and **< 100 mR/hr on contact**.

## Radiation Survey at Full Power

The RP Field Operations Group should perform an ionizing radiation survey around the klystron at its final operating level to check if sufficient lead shielding is installed to keep radiation below **5 mR/hr at 30 cm** away from any surface of the klystron and **< 100 mR/hr on contact**.
