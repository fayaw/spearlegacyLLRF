## RF Station Full Power Test & Survey

Survey performed by PEP-II RF Group.

To test the full power operation of the high voltage power supply and 1.2 MW klystron of a RF station a short circuit plate "SHORT" is installed in the waveguide between the circulator and the first magic tee power splitter. Power from the klystron is reflected by the SHORT back into the circulator and its 1.2 MW load.

Since this test does not put power into the RF cavities no ionizing radiation is produced in the tunnel. This survey only checks for non-ionizing radiation from the waveguide and any ionizing radiation from the klystron.

## Non-Ionizing RF Radiation Survey

With station turned on at a klystron output power level of approximately 100 kW a **RF RADIATION SURVEY** using a calibrated RF field meter has to be performed. The RF radiation level must be below **0.1 mW/cm²** anywhere around the accessible waveguide up to the position of the short.

This survey is to be repeated at the **full power level of the klystron of 1.2 MW** with a maximum allowable RF radiation level of **1.5 mW/cm²**.

## Ionizing Radiation Survey

An **IONIZING RADIATION SURVEY** around the klystron at full operating level is required to check if sufficient lead shielding is installed to keep radiation below **5 mR/hr** at 30 cm away from any surface of the klystron.
